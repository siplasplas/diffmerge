it for inclusion in DiffMerge, you agree to these terms.

Section 6 of LGPL-3.0 permits a library to name a proxy who can decide whether
future versions of the GNU Lesser General Public License may be used.
For DiffMerge, the proxy is its author, Andrzej Borucki, or a person he names
publicly as the proxy.

A later LGPL version is not authorized automatically. The proxy may accept it
by a public statement, such as a notice in this repository. That acceptance
permanently authorizes recipients to use DiffMerge, including contributions
submitted under these rules, under the accepted version as an alternative to
LGPL-3.0. The option to use LGPL-3.0 remains available.

By contributing, you agree that this proxy decision applies to your
contribution, without requiring separate consent from every contributor for
such a future LGPL version. This authorization concerns later LGPL versions,
not a change to an unrelated license. It does not change the licenses of
third-party code or dependencies.

## How to contribute

- Write code, comments, documentation and commit messages in English.
- Match the surrounding style and the repository conventions in `AGENTS.md`.
- Describe user-visible changes in `README.md`.
- Keep private data out of commits: keys, tokens, personal paths, e-mail
  addresses, logs and settings files.
- Preserve license notices and identify any third-party material you include.
